# Study Tracker navigation

Build a navigation bar for a study tracker.

It must show the brand name **Study Tracker** and links for **Dashboard**, **Subjects**, and **Stats**. On wide screens, keep the links visible. On narrow screens, provide a control that opens and closes the links. The control must work with a keyboard, and its accessible expanded state must match whether the links are open.

Check the result at wide and narrow viewport sizes. Keep the content readable and usable without horizontal overflow.
